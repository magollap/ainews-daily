Top Story

OpenAI and Meta each buy a coding-agent startup on the same day

OpenAI said it will acquire Pierre Computer Company, the team behind Code Storage and the open-source Trees and Diffs libraries, and plans to fold Code Storage into Codex. OpenAI says Pierre's open-source projects stay open and current Code Storage customers can keep using it; no price, closing date or timeline was given, and the deal still needs regulatory approval.

Hours earlier, Axios reported that Meta bought Conductor, a tool for running several coding agents (Claude Code, Codex and others) in parallel workspaces. Conductor's hosted cloud service is reported to shut down on November 20.

Both deals point at the same thing: with models converging, the big labs are buying the layer around the agent, meaning source control, orchestration and supervision of many agents at once.

Why it matters  If your product depends on a third-party agent harness, expect those tools to be absorbed or sunset. Keep your own orchestration portable and avoid hard dependencies on indie tooling that is now an acquisition target.

ChatGPT Images 2.6 adds GIFs and two new API image models

The update lets users create and edit animated GIFs and reuse reference images. In the API there are two new models: Flare, tuned for speed (about six seconds an image), and Sunburst, aimed at quality and consistent edits. Both accept markup-guided edits and can export SVG. Together AI · Nov 4

Together Compiler: agents that keep re-tuning inference

Together says its agentic compiler continuously rewrites GPU code as models, traffic and hardware change, and already serves trillions of tokens a day. It claims figures like 1,205 tokens/sec on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (GB300, batch size 1) and up to 50% faster customer workloads. SemiAnalysis says it validated one of the benchmark results; a TVM-team response argues hand-built DSLs still matter.

Why it matters  Lower latency per token directly cuts time-to-first-word for voice agents if you host open models.

Buoyant · Nov 5

Kinference goes GA: an inference control plane for Kubernetes

From the creators of Linkerd, Kinference sits on top of the vLLM engine and adds routing, load balancing and policy across models and GPUs. It picks endpoints using KV-cache and queue state, can classify prompts to send simple ones to smaller models, bursts to cloud models under set conditions, and attributes GPU cost per tenant. Buoyant's own benchmark claims a 95% cut in median time-to-first-token versus standard Kubernetes load balancing on one workload. Apache 2.0.

Why it matters  If you self-host speech or LLM models on K8s, cache-aware routing is the cheapest latency win available.

Google Cloud · CNCF listing confirmed Nov 5 (announced Nov 3)

Agent Substrate and gVisor head to the CNCF

Google plans to donate Agent Substrate, gVisor and OpenRL to the CNCF at Sandbox level. Substrate keeps each agent session's state (files, memory, network rules) as an "Actor" and lends it a warm Kubernetes worker only when it is running, snapshotting it when idle. Google claims restores in under 500 ms; that figure is unverified.

Why it matters  Sandboxed, suspend-and-resume agent runtimes are becoming a standard Kubernetes primitive.

7 · Voice AI corner Speech · realtime · voice agents

OpenAI · Nov 4

GPT-Live voice agents can now dial out

OpenAI made outbound calling generally available for GPT-Live over SIP trunks, with Telnyx and Twilio as launch partners and Ozonetel also supported. It supports HD voice and two-way DTMF, so an agent can both press and hear keypad tones to navigate phone menus. The model is $0.05 a minute, plus whatever your carrier charges. SIP joins the existing WebRTC and WebSocket transports.

Why it matters  A direct competitor to stacked platforms for outbound use cases; recheck your per-minute math against Retell/Vapi-style bundles and keep consent and recording disclosure in the flow.

ElevenLabs · Nov 5

ElevenMusic can publish straight to SoundCloud

ElevenLabs users can now post tracks made in ElevenMusic directly to SoundCloud, and SoundCloud Artist Pro members get three months of ElevenMusic Pro. The company says over 25 million tracks have been generated with its models. Separately, it became the first AI company to join the music-data standards body DDEX.

Why it matters  Shows ElevenLabs widening from voice into audio distribution; useful context if you build on its voices.

Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash reaches general availability

Clips can now run up to 15 seconds (up from 10), with closer adherence to image and video references, better lip-sync and conversational editing. Drafts render at 360p/720p and upscale to 1080p or 4K. Price is $0.07 per second at 720p, 30% below the previous version; outputs carry a SynthID watermark. Artlist, Krea, Picsart, Runway and others are building on it.

Why it matters  Per-second pricing at this level makes personalized video follow-ups after a call realistic.
