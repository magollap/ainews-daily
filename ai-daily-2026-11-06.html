OpenAI Unveils GPT-6 Cyber, a Vulnerability-Hunting Model Kept Behind a Locked Door

OpenAI announced a cybersecurity-specialised model on Nov 5 (called GPT-6 Cyber by Fortune and "ChatGPT Cyber" by TechCrunch) and is limiting it to the top tier of its Daybreak Red program, which serves offensive security researchers. OpenAI says the same model scanned the Linux kernel and flagged 948 potential issues the day before, and turned up 81 novel vulnerabilities in Firefox.

The launch comes with a $1 billion initiative for defenders protecting critical infrastructure such as grids and water systems. TechCrunch reports the Linux bug hunt alone cost about $1.2 million in compute, and researchers in the program complain that defenders are rationed while attackers can run uncensored open-weight models.

Why it matters: Frontier labs are now gating their strongest capabilities by use case and identity checks. Expect the same tiered-access pattern for any agent that can act on production systems, including voice agents with tool access.

1 · New Claude features

Personal Finance arrives in Claude

Anthropic began rolling out a beta that lets Pro, Max and Ultra users in the US link bank, card, loan and investment accounts (via Plaid) and ask questions about their own spending. Access is read-only, Claude cannot move money or give investment advice, and Anthropic says there are no ads or sponsored recommendations. It can categorise spending, flag duplicate charges and chart bills.

Why it matters: A clear template for consented, read-only data connections in a consumer assistant, with trust and compliance baked in.

Enterprise gets a security center and inference hooks

Claude's release notes for Nov 5 list new Enterprise security and admin controls: a security center, inference hooks, centralised management and extra access controls, several still in beta. This week's Platform notes also mention an org IP allowlist (beta) and an Admin API call to sign members out everywhere.

Why it matters: Inference hooks are the kind of interception point teams need for logging, redaction and policy checks on agent traffic.

3 · LLM & model updates

Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash goes GA with longer, cheaper video

Google's updated video model is available in the Gemini API and for business, rolling out to Gemini app subscribers. Clips run up to 15 seconds (from 10), pricing falls 30% to $0.07 per second at 720p, and drafts can be upscaled to 1080p or 4K. Google cites better reference-following, steadier faces and packaging text, and cleaner lip-sync.

Why it matters: Cheap draft-then-upscale makes iterating on avatar or ad video affordable inside a product.
