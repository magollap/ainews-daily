Product · Beta

Claude can now read your bank accounts

Anthropic is rolling out a Personal Finance beta to US Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers on web, desktop, iOS and Android. Users link accounts through Plaid; Claude gets read-only access to balances, credit limits and up to 24 months of transactions, and can turn them into charts, calendars and dashboards. It cannot move money.

Anthropic says chats inside the Personal Finance hub are not used for training. Disconnecting a bank stops future reads but does not erase figures already in chats, artifacts or shared links.

Why it matters  It is a template for consented, connector-based access to sensitive data. If you build voice agents that touch customer accounts, copy the read-only scope, the visible unlink flow and the explicit retention rules.

Security · Claude Code

"Don't ask again" leaked secrets into public GitHub repos

Security firm Apiiro found that choosing "Yes, and don't ask again" in Claude Code can store the exact approved command, secrets included, in plaintext in .claude/settings.local.json. Across public GitHub it counted 1,122 files with a real secret (1,379 distinct secrets), among them database passwords, Supabase keys, SSH logins and AWS keys. Counting older file versions and transcripts, the tally reached 2,539 across 1,994 repos.

Claude Code lead Boris Cherny said on Hacker News that the team is looking at secret scanning and auto-mode checks, though he conceded these may not fully fix it. Apiiro advises rotating any credential the file ever held.

Why it matters  Audit your repos for that file today, gitignore it, and approve command patterns rather than literal commands. Never type secrets inline in agent-run shell commands.

Anthropic reportedly spends ~$3.4B on gas generators for data centers

The FT reported Sunday (via Reuters) that Anthropic bought commitments for 1.7 GW of heavy-duty gas generators from Texas supplier OnePWR Solutions in late September. It did not acquire OnePWR or its staff, but the deal includes the engineering and construction capability to install them. Reuters could not verify the report and Anthropic did not comment. Why it matters  Power is now a bottleneck Anthropic is buying its way around. Expect capacity, rate limits and pricing to track that build-out.

Security · Hacker News

ChatGPT Desktop reportedly forwards your SSH agent to remote hosts

A Hacker News post flags that ChatGPT Desktop silently enables SSH agent forwarding to hosts it connects to, so a Codex agent running there could authenticate as you, including pushing to private GitHub repos. Commenters link the behavior to openai/codex PR #28131 and criticise the lack of per-signature approval. OpenAI has not responded in the sources I found.

Why it matters  Same lesson as the Claude Code story: agent convenience features keep leaking credentials. Review what your agent hosts can forward.

Policy · Enterprise

Microsoft starts undisclosed Copilot Chat caps on 2,000+ seat tenants

From Nov 9, Microsoft applies fair-use limits on more Copilot Chat features, including image generation, to unlicensed users in tenants above 2,000 seats, after smaller tenants on Nov 2. The numeric limits are unpublished and admins cannot see or change them.

Why it matters  Opaque quotas are a reliability risk. Build for graceful degradation when a vendor throttles without telling you the number.

Benchmarks

Artificial Analysis ships Decision Index v2

Released Nov 8, v2 raises the weight of private benchmarks from 25% to 45% and rewards calibrated confidence as well as accuracy. Decision-specialist models Drex Max v1.1 and Fastino GLiDE v1.2 tie at 73. On the general LLM side, Claude Haiku 5.5 (Max) leads at 77, with GPT-6 Luna (Max) at 76 at higher cost and latency.

Why it matters  A cheap small model topping a decision benchmark is a reason to re-test your routing. Vendor-supplied scores are only a starting point.

Meta says Muse and Meta AI now run on new specialized models trained using "Muse Wonder", bringing Active Memory and Active Focus to everyone. Meta claims they are 2.4x less likely to forget critical info and 67% better at self-correcting. The Times separately reports a UK Muse launch in coming weeks with retailer purchase partnerships; Muse has passed 11 million users.

Research

Editing 1,023 weights lifts Qwen by 29 points on MATH-500

A Shanghai AI Lab, Tongji and Fudan paper studies "weight-space programming", where a reasoning agent edits a model's parameters directly instead of training by gradient. On Qwen3.5 models it reports gains up to 29.45 points on MATH-500, with one 1,023-parameter edit giving that lift. Generalization was uneven, and 6 of 16 trajectories reverted to the original weights when edits could not be validated.

Chips

Google adds two TPUs: 8tx and 8i Max

Announced Nov 9, the TPU 8tx pairs the 8t silicon with 5th Gen AMD EPYC hosts for confidential computing and claims up to 2x lower latency than 8t. The 8i Max is a dual-chip package that doubles compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth over the 8i for long-context inference. Alphabet rose over 2% premarket.

Why it matters  More inference silicon options for long-context and confidential workloads, and more pricing pressure on GPU-only stacks.

Legal & safety

Meta expands AI age estimation to Instagram globally; Asahi Linux drops MIT license

Meta will use AI cues such as apparent height and bone structure to estimate whether users are teens, as lawsuits mount (Reuters). Separately, Asahi Linux moved all repos from MIT to GPLv2-or-later after bad actors used LLMs to rewrite code lifted from contributors' forks and strip attribution.

GKE · GA

GKE dynamic slicing for TPUs is generally available

Google says dynamic slicing decouples logical TPU slices from node pools, cutting AI workload startup by up to 5x and recovery by 4.5x. It supports super-slicing (stitching 16-VM TPU cubes into larger slices) and sub-slicing (partitioning a cube for several jobs). It requires pre-provisioned 16-VM node pools and TPU reservation mode.

Why it matters  Faster recovery from hardware faults means less lost training and serving time on TPUs.

Voice AI corner Short

Models

Shizuku-Duplex: a Japanese full-duplex voice model

Shizuku AI released a full-duplex model that listens while it speaks, runs inference every 80 ms, and can nod along, interject and reply before the user finishes. It speaks a quick holding answer while a web search runs, then gives the detailed result, and it summarizes history every five minutes to stay stable. In the company's tests it ranked second among full-duplex models behind GPT-Live-1.

Why it matters  Turn-taking is the quality gap in voice agents, and full-duplex plus filler-while-searching is a pattern worth copying.
